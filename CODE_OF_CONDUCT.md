# Code of conduct

Everyone who works on Document Intelligence (code, documents, issues and reviews) is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent;
- give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person;
- keep customer documents and data out of issues, commits and chat: use the bundled sample invoices to show a
  problem;
- report unacceptable behaviour through the support channel in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules.
